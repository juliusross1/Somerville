2. You have previously share with me that you are a user of accessibility technology.  Within your comfort zone can you tell me about your use of such technology, in particular in relation to how you use it in your role as a mathematician.

(a) Can you walk me through how you read a mathematical paper?  

(ai) What makes a paper easy for you to read?

(b) Can you walk me through how you write a mathematical paper or document?

(c) Walk me through how you collaborate with others on mathematics?  Have any of these issues influenced what you work on, what conferences you go to, who you work with?

(ci) What makes somebody an easy collaborator?

(d) Idea of invisible work that might have to be done before you can being your mathematical work.  Is that something you experience, and if so how much of that work might be invisible to your colleagues?

3. Have you developed a way of thinking about or doing mathematics that colleagues might not have encountered?

4. How effective is what is currently available.  

(a) What parts are working well?
(b) What parts need improvement?

5. Have you see a change in the coverage of accessibility during your time using these (either in terms of how available it is, or how effective it is)?

6. Moving away from technology, what challenges remain regarding accessibility within mathematics more widely?

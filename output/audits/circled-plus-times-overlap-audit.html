Measurement definitions and validation

The component dependencies were resolved bottom-up using native Glyphs 4.203 in a temporary font copy. Native component translations, rotations, scales and smart-component values were applied. The source was not saved or modified. Each resolved glyph consists of the outer circle contour, inner circle contour and two rectangular bars.

Each bar has two short end edges, giving four arm tips. A passing tip has its entire end edge outside the inner contour, at a minimum Euclidean separation of at least 2 font units, and remains inside or on the outer contour. An end edge crossing the inner contour has only partial overlap and cannot provide a 2-unit minimum over its full width. A fully internal end edge has no overlap; the table reports its nearest gap to the inner contour, not a negative overlap depth. Exterior protrusion is the greatest distance of an end corner outside the outer contour. All outer contours passed a convexity check, so checking all polygon vertices establishes containment of the entire bars.

Distances are measured to the cubic Bézier contours, rather than just their bounding boxes or centre lines. Curve/point minima use roots of the squared-distance derivative; curve/segment minima include line intersections, perpendicular extrema and endpoints. Analytic reference cases passed. Protruding corners were independently checked against densely subdivided curves; the maximum difference was below 0.000060 units. A numerical comparison tolerance of 0.00001 units is used at zero and the 2-unit threshold. No flags are borderline at that tolerance.

Coverage counts refer to stored layers and all enumerated variants, not every possible interpolated location between font masters.

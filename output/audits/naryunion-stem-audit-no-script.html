Union and square-union stem audit

Current saved source: sources/SomervilleMath.glyphspackage. Script and scriptscript glyphs are excluded. Checks cover the 16 matching drawing layers of the active union and square bowls, their plain and serif assemblies, and both cap glyphs. The component-only size variants inherit these drawings; their intermediate smart-component interpolation is not independently rendered. Inactive backup layers and the unused legacy bowl are excluded. No glyph changes made. Component placement checks use stored transforms, without invoking live Glyphs automatic alignment.

All values are font units. U = union; S = square union. Positive x offsets mean rightward. High means the smart-component height-100 layer. “Center vs stems” compares center.x with the midpoint of the two stem centres. “Center vs advance” compares center.x with half the advance width. Sidebearings here use the outer stem edges, excluding serif overhangs. Differences between U and S may be intentional; they are reported separately from geometric faults.
